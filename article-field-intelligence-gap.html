The Field Intelligence Gap Is Killing Your Competitive Edge

Darren Pollock, Founder — ConnectRx 6 min read August 2026

The Problem

Every medical device sales rep in the country is sitting on a goldmine of intelligence they can't share.

A competitor launches a new product at your flagship account. A surgeon mentions frustration with their current device. A supply chain issue starts affecting theatre scheduling. A pricing shift at a rival distributor just changed the tender landscape.

Your reps see all of it. Every day. In hospital corridors, between appointments, over coffee with clinical staff.

And then? It dies.

It dies in WhatsApp group chats, buried under 47 other messages. It dies in emails that get forwarded once and forgotten. It dies in reps' heads because there's no fast way to capture it and no system to make it matter.

According to Crayon's 2026 State of Competitive Intelligence report — their ninth annual edition, surveying hundreds of companies across industries — field-generated intelligence has overtaken competitor websites as the single most-cited source of competitive intelligence. 54% of teams now rank internal data, call insights, and frontline observations as their top source.

Yet here's the gap that should keep every sales leader awake: most organisations still have no systematic way to capture it.

Crayon found that only 56% of teams share competitive intelligence weekly or faster. The rest are on monthly, quarterly, or worse. And the difference is staggering.

  • Teams sharing intelligence weekly or faster achieve positive revenue impact at 79%.
  • Teams on a monthly-or-slower cadence: 41%.

Frequency, not format, is what separates winners from the rest.

I've spent 25 years in medical device and pharma sales. I've watched this gap cost companies deals, market share, and their best reps' knowledge when those reps walk out the door. That's why I built GekoPulse.

"Intelligence has a half-life. And yours is expiring."

The Solution

GekoPulse is a purpose-built competitive field intelligence platform designed for the way reps actually work.

Voice-first capture. A rep finishes a customer visit, opens the app, and records a 10-second voice memo. No forms. No CRM fields. No typing in a hospital corridor. OpenAI Whisper transcribes it instantly. Photo capture handles competitor displays, product issues, or clinical evidence when voice isn't practical.

AI analysis and categorisation. Each submission is automatically sorted — Competitive, Clinical, Opportunity, Customer, Logistics. Urgency-scored so critical alerts surface immediately. Sentiment-detected so shifts in customer mood are tracked before they become problems. Theme-clustered so related insights across the team emerge as patterns.

Human validation. The AI proposes. The rep confirms. Nothing goes live without human sign-off. This isn't a black box — it's a co-pilot that respects the rep's judgment.

Manager Command Centre. A real-time dashboard showing trending topics, critical alerts, team activity, and emerging themes. Managers can acknowledge alerts, flag insights for action, and push intelligence back to the field in real time.

AI-suggested questions. GekoPulse analyses trending themes and generates targeted questions for reps to explore in their next conversations — creating a continuous intelligence feedback loop that gets sharper with every submission.

Gamification. XP, levels, submission streaks. Reps compete to contribute the best intelligence, not just the most. Capture becomes an achievement, not an obligation.

Why It Matters

The Crayon report reveals another divide that's widening fast: AI agents.

Teams running AI agents in production or pilot achieve revenue impact at 82%, versus 42% for teams that don't. That's one of the widest gaps in the entire survey. GekoPulse puts AI to work where it matters — not writing content, but running the intelligence loop: capturing, analysing, surfacing, suggesting.

"The teams winning at competitive intelligence don't have better information. They have a better system for capturing it, distributing it, and acting on it — weekly, not quarterly."

Crayon's blueprint is clear: get structured, get on a weekly clock, put AI to work. That's not a roadmap we followed — it's the problem we built GekoPulse to solve.

GekoPulse runs at a fraction of the cost of Veeva, Salesforce, or IQVIA — with introductory partner pricing for early teams. Not because it does less, but because it's built for the gap they don't address: real-time field intelligence capture, AI-analysed and distributed before the moment to act has passed.

The Takeaway

Your field reps are your highest-value intelligence asset. Crayon just proved it with data.

The question isn't whether your reps are seeing competitive intelligence worth capturing. They are. The question is whether you have a system to capture it, analyse it, and act on it before it expires.

If your competitive intelligence lives in WhatsApp threads and quarterly reviews, you're losing deals you don't even know are competitive.

Capture. Analyse. Act. That's not a tagline. It's the operating model Crayon's data says separates winners from the rest. And it's what GekoPulse does from day one.
