Common Questions

Competitive field intelligence,
explained.

What is competitive field intelligence?
Competitive field intelligence is the intelligence generated by frontline sales reps in the field — competitor launches, pricing moves, clinician sentiment, supply disruptions and market dynamics — captured and analysed systematically so leadership can act on it. In Crayon's 2026 State of Competitive Intelligence report, field-generated intelligence overtook competitor websites as the single most-cited source of competitive intelligence, cited by 54% of teams.
How is GekoPulse different from a CRM like Veeva or Salesforce?
Legacy CRMs record what already happened. GekoPulse is built for the gap they don't address: capturing competitive field intelligence in real time. A rep records a 10-second voice memo, AI transcribes and categorises it, urgency-scores it and clusters it with related insights across the team, and it surfaces on a manager dashboard immediately — not at next quarter's review. It runs at a fraction of the cost of Veeva, Salesforce or IQVIA, with introductory partner pricing for early teams.
How do medical device sales teams capture field intelligence with GekoPulse?
Reps capture intelligence by voice, text or photo straight after a customer visit — no forms, no CRM fields. AI transcribes and proposes a category (Competitive, Clinical, Opportunity, Customer, Logistics), an urgency score and a sentiment reading. The rep confirms, and the insight is live on the manager command centre in seconds.
Does GekoPulse replace the sales rep's judgement with AI?
No. The AI proposes; the rep confirms. Nothing goes live without human sign-off. GekoPulse is a co-pilot for the intelligence loop — capturing, analysing, surfacing and suggesting — while the rep stays in control of what is recorded.
