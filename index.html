Field Intelligence

GekoPulse

Your real-time strategic command centre. Reps capture critical field intelligence via voice, text or photo — AI summarises and categorises it instantly, pushing insights to a company-wide dashboard so teams stay agile and ahead.

  • AI categorisation & trending topics dashboard, visible company-wide
  • Managers broadcast intel back to the field — AI generates suggested discovery questions
  • Strategy & tactic formulation dashboard — feeds directly into GekoConnect

Strategic Sales

GekoConnect

The forward-looking strategic account manager. Visual customer mapping across fully customisable Use, Access & Importance parameters — with gamified OKRs, AI coaching, and influence network mapping that turns tacit rep knowledge into company-wide intelligence.

Why ConnectRx

Built differently,
for a reason.

Generic CRMs record history. ConnectRx is built to drive what happens next — with tools designed from the ground up for complex, relationship-driven sales environments.

Visual Account Mapping
Rank every HCP across Use, Access and Importance. Surface KOLs. Map influence networks. Turn a rep's tacit knowledge into a shared, visual strategic asset.
Proactive, Not Reactive
Most CRMs record what happened. GekoConnect is built around where you're going — OKRs, customer plans, and strategies defined before the visit, not after.
Real-Time Command Centre
GekoPulse pushes field intelligence to every level of the organisation the moment it's captured. Managers can respond, broadcast guidance and generate reports — all in real time.
AI as Coach, Not Controller
AI suggests questions, objectives and tactics — but reps accept, reject or modify every suggestion. The technology accelerates human judgement; it doesn't replace it.
Gamification That Drives Execution
GekoConnect uses a gamified OKR approach — psychology of colour, engagement mechanics, and clear progress signals — to make executing a sales strategy as compelling as it is effective.
Mobile-First. Not an Afterthought.
Built for iPhone and iPad from day one — not a desktop interface crammed onto a small screen. Reps in the field get the same power as managers at headquarters.
