Common Questions

Competitive field intelligence,
explained.

What is competitive field intelligence?
Competitive field intelligence is the intelligence generated by frontline sales teams in the field — competitor launches, pricing moves, clinician sentiment, supply disruptions and market dynamics — captured and analysed systematically so leadership can act on it. In Crayon's 2026 State of Competitive Intelligence report, field-generated intelligence overtook competitor websites as the single most-cited source of competitive intelligence, cited by 54% of teams.
How is GekoPulse different from a CRM like Veeva or Salesforce?
Legacy CRMs record what already happened. GekoPulse is built for the gap they don't address: capturing competitive field intelligence in real time. A team member records a 10-second voice memo, AI transcribes and categorises it, urgency-scores it and clusters it with related insights across the team, and it surfaces on a manager dashboard immediately — not at next quarter's review. It runs at a fraction of the cost of Veeva, Salesforce or IQVIA, with introductory partner pricing for early teams.
How do sales teams capture field intelligence with GekoPulse?
Your team captures intelligence by voice, text or photo straight after a customer visit — no forms, no CRM fields. AI transcribes and proposes a category (Competitive, Clinical, Opportunity, Customer, Logistics), an urgency score and a sentiment reading. They confirm, and the insight is live on the manager command centre in seconds.
Does GekoPulse replace the sales professional's judgement with AI?
No. The AI proposes; the team member confirms. Nothing goes live without human sign-off. GekoPulse is a co-pilot for the intelligence loop — capturing, analysing, surfacing and suggesting — while the team member stays in control of what is recorded.
How does GekoPulse make sure my team actually adopts it?
Every GekoPulse rollout runs through a six-week onboarding programme led by ConnectRx, in which we treat your sales team as the customer to be won. We work alongside managers and teams to embed the capture habit, get the manager command centre into the weekly rhythm, and report adoption throughout. By week six the habit is established and management has a clear picture of what the field is surfacing. More on the programme.
