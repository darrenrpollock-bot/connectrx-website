2.3 Information about other people in field notes

Field notes are about the medical-device market: competitor products and activity, customer feedback and what happens at hospitals and clinics. Notes can therefore include the names of people such as clinicians, hospital staff or competitor sales reps (for example, "Dr Alex Morgan at Example Private Hospital is trialling a competitor's device"). Voice recordings and photos can also capture people in the background by accident.

We ask users to record only what's needed for business purposes, and not to photograph or record people who haven't agreed. [TO CONFIRM: lawyer to review this section and the APP 5 position, see open question 14.]

2.4 Patient and health information

GekoPulse is not designed for patient information or health information, and users must not enter it. That includes patient names, dates of birth, Medicare numbers, medical record numbers and details of anyone's health or treatment.

Because notes are captured in clinical settings, patient details may still be captured by accident. When the app detects likely patient details in a transcript or typed note, it tries to mask them (for example, replacing a name with initials) before the note is analysed, saved to the main record or shown in the app. This automatic masking can miss things. The original voice or text may already have been sent to our transcription or AI provider (see section 4) before masking.
